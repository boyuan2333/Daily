| Route switch | From Planning, activate a different draft or paused route while another route is active. Close and restart. | The old route has a return anchor, and restart restores only the newly active route. |
| Paused route visibility | Keep one route active and at least two previously started routes paused. | Exact paused-route resume | Resume a paused route that was stopped partway through, then return to Guide. | Guide shows the exact retained action. The user is not asked to choose a step or reconstruct context. |
